The split

Tax assumptions used

Salaries tax is annualised (×12) and computed as the lower of the progressive scale — 2%, 6%, 10%, 14%, 17% on $50,000 bands above the basic allowance — or the standard rate of 15% on net income up to $5m, 16% above. MPF at 5% is deductible, capped at $18,000/year. Assumes a full year of Hong Kong employment, no dependants, no other deductions, and that you were not in the year of assessment 2025/26 tax reduction. Guidance only, not tax advice — check your IRD assessment.
